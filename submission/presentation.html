Problem Statement

PS-06 On-device personalized learning assistant

Turning student study materials into offline-first grounded tutoring, dynamic quizzes, and personalized learning paths without private cloud dependencies.

The Student Problem
SLIDE 02 / 12

Critical Learning Challenges Solved

  • Disconnection from Course Materials: Generic AI models provide generic answers disconnected from actual course syllabi and teacher slides.
  • The Hallucination Trap: Language models invent non-existent page numbers and formulas, misleading students during exam preparation.
  • Connectivity Gaps: Cloud-dependent tutors fail completely during commutes or in areas with spotty internet connectivity.
  • Lack of Adaptive Memory: Students waste time reviewing concepts they already know while neglecting critical prerequisite gaps.
Solution: Grounded Learning Loop
SLIDE 03 / 12

The Closed-Loop Pedagogical Pipeline

Student Material → Local Ingestion & Chunking → Room SQLite Knowledge Base → BM25 Retrieval → Grounded Socratic Tutor → Diagnostic Assessment → Skill Mastery Tracking → SM-2 Review Scheduling

Architecture & System Stack
SLIDE 08 / 12

Production Android Architecture

  • UI: 100% Jetpack Compose with Material Design 3, dynamic theme support, and responsive layouts.
  • State Management: Single-activity architecture, Android ViewModel, Kotlin Coroutines, and reactive StateFlow.
  • Persistence: Room SQLite with KSP annotation processor and robust DAOs.
  • Retrieval: In-process BM25 Okapi lexical engine executing queries in under 50ms.
