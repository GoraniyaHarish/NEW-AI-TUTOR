THE PROBLEM

Why Generic Study Tools Fall Short

The real pain points students face with existing educational tools.

📄

1. Information Overload & Disorganization

Students juggle hundreds of lecture PDF slides, problem sets, and syllabi without an automated understanding of prerequisite dependencies.

⚠️

2. Generic AI Hallucinations

Standard chatbots don't know the student's exact course syllabus. They invent formulas, introduce foreign notations, or explain concepts out of exam scope.

📶

3. Cloud Dependency & Network Fragility

Students commuting, studying in offline libraries, or in low-connectivity areas completely lose access to cloud-only learning assistants.

❌
The Passive Reading Trap

Re-reading notes gives an illusion of competence. Without targeted diagnostic quizzes, spaced repetition, and prerequisite tracking, students fail to retain core concepts.

• No offline access
• No grounded citations
• Zero prerequisite mapping

OUR WORKING SOLUTION

Meet LearnMate: Grounded Personal Tutor

A native Android app turning raw student materials into an adaptive mastery graph.

📥

1. Material Ingestion & Chunking (Working)

Extracts PDF text and notes into 400–600 token chunks with 50-token overlap stored safely in local Room Database.

🕸️

2. Automated Skill Map & DAG (Working)

Extracts course skills, maps prerequisite dependencies, and visualizes mastery states (Not Assessed, Needs Attention, Learning, Strong).

🔍

3. Grounded Retrieval & AI Tutor (Working)

Answers student questions by strictly citing document name and page number. Zero hallucinated references.

5. Objective Mathematical Mastery Engine

Multi-factor heuristic and Exponential Moving Average (EMA) progress scoring.

🧮

The Blended Mastery Equation

NewMastery = (0.6 × QuizScore) + (0.4 × PrevMastery)

First attempts take 100% of quiz score directly to establish clean baselines.

⚖️

Difficulty Modifiers & Hint Penalties

Questions scale by difficulty (Easy ×0.85, Medium ×1.0, Hard ×1.2). Using hints applies a -4% penalty to encourage independent recall.

🔥

Consecutive Streak Bonuses

Consecutive correct answers add up to +10% motivation bonus without skewing genuine mastery.

Mastery Evaluation Multi-Factors
